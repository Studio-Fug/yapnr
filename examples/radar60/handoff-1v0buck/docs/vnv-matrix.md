## Approved exceptions and owner decisions

These are in `docs/design/board-design.md` §0.1, its appended D14/D15 notes, and
`docs/stage3c/plan.md`:

- **D6:** TX feed-loss waiver up to 2.7 dB.
- **D14:** the macro owns the VOUT_PA feed; coupling rule ≤ −40 dB.
- **D15:** stitching study; A chosen.
- **Dummies S1** (outer only).
- **R46:** becomes a WFCP0612 1 mΩ part (default).
- **Local sensing:** the 0.5 mΩ 1V0_BUCK/1V0_SH budgets are reported as quantified warnings if only
  those legs fail.
- **6 layers** unless the 8-layer criteria trigger and the owner confirms.
- **ANT-02:** the RL/gain/efficiency misses are preregistered under the D5 rule.
- **LVDS uncoupled limit:** resolved to 3 mm (2026-10-06, this handoff).
