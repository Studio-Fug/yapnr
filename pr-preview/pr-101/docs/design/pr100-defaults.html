PR #100 default activation and validation¶

All production features introduced by PR #100 are enabled by default. Each Boolean engine switch accepts an explicit 0 to restore its ablation. Electrical limits, KiCad DRC, total differential-pair contracts and evidence requirements are unchanged.

Switch

Behavior

PNR_HIER_SAFE_PARTITION

Preserve hard relationships across block partitioning

PNR_NATIVE_OUTLINE

Preserve the actual board contour and reject inconsistent dimensions

PNR_POWER_BRANCHES

Cover every carrying branch and require native power screening

PNR_POWER_CHANNELS

Retain channel demand for shared power nets

PNR_PAD_LOCALITY

Attract small components to the actual connected IC pads

PNR_LEGALIZE_SHOVE

Transactional neighbor displacement with complete legality checks

PNR_PLACEMENT_BANKS

Honor authored capacitor banks and their maximum span

PNR_SMPS_LAYOUT

Run declared SMPS checks and retain unknown qualification

PNR_SMPS_PLACEMENT

Include declared SMPS geometric debt in placement

PNR_INGEST_COPPER_PADS

Exclude mask/paste-only apertures from copper-pad occupancy

PNR_PLANE_ACCESS_COMPACT_BUS

Keep the full-width collector within its via-bank span

PNR_PLANE_ACCESS_NATIVE_FIT

Judge current-sized access with native geometry instead of symmetric courtyard inflation

PNR_HIER_LAYOUT_DIVERSE

Preserve distinct valid block layouts in the search portfolio

PNR_HIER_LAYOUT_SEARCH defaults to alternating: bounded variant swaps and parent relaxation. off, independent, frozen, and paired remain selectable comparison strategies. Mutually exclusive strategies are not run simultaneously. The existing fixed evaluation and iteration limits remain active.

New placement-lab runs enable shove and authored banks in the browser, API and CLI. --no-shove and --no-placement-banks opt out. Replay of older captures preserves their recorded feature settings. Unlimited interactive running, legalize-only replay and diagnostic captures remain explicit user actions; they are execution modes, not disabled production repairs.

Native ladder comparison¶

The full eight-case ladder at seeds 0 and 1 passes 16/16 on main and 16/16 with these defaults. Both arms have zero native unconnected items and DRC findings; source-width, contact and placement checks pass. Identical four-round and 600-second stage budgets were used. Main is 9e19d73e; candidate is c50ffdfc, with the documented defaults, scalar transaction cache, hierarchy-spacing and fanout clearance fixes. Seeds and frozen source hashes are retained in the experiment evidence.

Native showcases¶

All five showcases pass on main and c50ffdfc, including the hierarchical twin-bank design. Aggregate copper falls from 1233.55 to 1143.86 mm (7.3%) and vias from 79 to 75. Total measured stage time rises from 246.35 to 249.94 seconds (1.5%); place/route time rises from 96.43 to 123.26 seconds (27.8%). CPU time is 424.86 versus 419.64 seconds; campaign elapsed is 268.62 versus 270.31 seconds. Timings were measured on a shared development machine.

This same-outline, seed-0 comparison shows the final saved copper. Both boards have zero native opens and DRC findings. Opposing bank orientations reduce copper from 487.01 to 443.60 mm, with 41 versus 43 vias. Layer-by-layer visual review found no apparent copper or placement defect. Nearby vias were checked against actual contacts; proximity alone was not used to declare redundancy. Board and copper hashes identify the rendered artifacts.

Scheduled hard regressions¶

All 20 scheduled hard cases pass on both arms at seed 0: the six public hard cases and fourteen nightly variants, including four-, six- and eight-layer chasers, double-sided and fixed-placement cases, arc-preserving copper, MCU USB, quad banks, power switches, buck pours and both UFBGA201 fanout variants. There are zero native opens, DRC findings or failed runner qualification gates.

The initial defaults exhausted the stage limit after one round without a final native board; unavailable results are not zero or passes. The scalar channel cache preserves directional summation order and skips only pairs whose original contributions are zero. All eight initial placements retain identical poses and scores while their combined time falls from 1342.447 to 385.657 seconds. Main’s initial pool took 113.548 seconds. The enabled repairs still have a placement cost, but the complete four-round search now fits the unchanged effort limit.

The new placement exposed an escape-halo defect: pad halos reserve touched cells, but fanout tails reserve cell centers. Reusing the pad-style track + pitch threshold let a 0.25 mm supply track approach a 0.10 mm tail to 0.04 mm against a 0.10 mm rule. Width-specific tables now protect intermediate-width tracks too. A coarse-grid guard keeps their halo radii nonnegative. Regression tests cover both outer layers, every surviving cardinal/diagonal edge and three grid pitches. Native artifact and crop metadata identify the unchanged geometry shown above; pads and planes are omitted from this track/via diagnostic crop.

The corrected engine refuses the unsafe RMII_MDIO crossing, leaving that net unrouted with two native open items. It has not found a clean alternate route. The net and acceptance gates remain present. All three differential-pair qualification failures also persist. Fewer skew findings and no new clearance violations come with more opens: this is mixed evidence, not routing completion.

The last actual SoC run used 47924da5. Its mapping to c50ffdfc is explicit: the only subsequent runtime change clamps a negative halo extent to zero. The SoC’s 0.25 mm grid, minimum 0.10 mm track width and minimum 0.10 mm clearance give a strictly positive minimum extent of 0.025 mm, so this guard is inactive. Exact source/AST differences, inputs, seeds and native output hashes are retained.

Validation scope¶

The scheduled native comparison totals 41/41 passes per arm: sixteen ladder runs, five showcases and twenty hard runs. Frozen candidate runtime sources match c50ffdfc. Across those runs, copper falls 5.9%, vias 6.4%, all-stage time 7.7% and CPU time 6.9%; place/route phase time falls 0.6%. The sixteen other manual hard variants are not included. 12-soc-bga-113 belongs to the manual stress inventory and is evaluated separately with a disclosed reduced budget; it is not included in the 41 passing runs. Per-case results, seeds and native board hashes retain the complete scheduled comparison.

All 310 regular Bazel test targets pass locally and in both Linux and macOS CI on c50ffdfc, along with all-file hooks, container/wheel builds on both architectures, native CI ladder checks, 27 browser tests and native current-sized collector regressions at four rotations. The separate RF nightly configuration passes 24 targets per platform using content-cached results; no new RF solver execution is claimed. The supplemental native inventory selects 119 methods across 22 modules: 118 pass and one is skipped because the local KiCad interpreter lacks PyTorch for the bottom-side footprint-point check. A duplicate full-PnR gloss test is excluded; the scheduled ladder separately exercises that pipeline. Negative native open/short controls pass. The four energy-controller tests use private Shapely 2.0.7 because local KiCad embeds Python 3.9; packaged Shapely 2.1.2 requires newer Python. No package pin was changed or exact 2.1.2 native-energy result claimed. The documented pre-existing manual detail-route, retry-controller and progress/placement-budget fixture tests are outside the regular 310-target lane and are not claimed as passes.

Expanded native testing also fixes two test-harness defects: the energy fixture no longer passes the controller Python’s standard-library paths to KiCad’s different Python version, and the library-table test honors the explicit headless CLI override with bounded subprocesses. Production runtime is unchanged by those test fixes.

Full-placement region/alignment and hard-edge integration tests needed appropriate Bazel timeout classes on shared CI runners (900 and 300 seconds respectively). Their iterations, seeds and assertions are unchanged, as are production budgets.

The hierarchy correction uses the legalizer’s authored courtyard gap instead of incorrectly adding copper clearance; positive and negative spacing regressions retain the original native copper rules. Failed search seeds retain diagnostics and allow the caller to try its remaining declared seeds. Native edge tests cover preserved contours by default and rectangular stamping with the explicit opt-out.

Final native boards have paired clean and annotated layer review. Forty of the 41 final candidate boards match the previously reviewed geometry exactly after removing only UUIDs and normalizing top-level serialization order. Exact byte hashes remain recorded alongside semantic comparisons. The changed MCU USB board and representative two-, four- and six-layer and hierarchical boards were freshly exported and reviewed on the final runtime. Nearby vias were checked against actual pad/track contacts and reviewed planes, including blind/buried transitions; close spacing alone does not prove redundant copper. Some fabrication labels remain crowded or extend outside the outline in synthetic fixtures. Long signal detours remain possible. These observations do not replace native DRC or electrical qualification.

The clean code suite and scheduled routing passes do not establish that every board is routed or electrically qualified. The SMPS article remains unqualified as recorded in the closure investigation. Final platform CI and the separate manual stress outcome are recorded on PR #100.
