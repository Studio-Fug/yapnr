# About the name

## yet another place and route

**yapnr** stands for "yet another place and route". Its slightly fantastical sound suggested a
second, playful expansion: "Yavanna-Assisted Placement 'N Routing". That backronym was coined for
this project.

The namesake is Yavanna, the figure in Tolkien's legendarium associated with growing things and the
protection of plants ([Tolkien Gateway](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Yavanna)). Her connection
to trees inspired the logo's central idea: a tree whose branches are electronic routes.

## The story

Imagine a gardener of silicon. First, each part finds its place; then paths grow between them. A
floorplan becomes a small, carefully tended forest of connections. The engine's practical work,
placing parts and routing signals, becomes the quiet craft of giving a circuit room to grow.

"Yet another place and route" remains the everyday expansion. The Yavanna backronym gives the
project a little woodland mythology to carry alongside it.

## The mark

The emblem is a compact circuit-tree sigil. Its branching trunk suggests a Y for yapnr. Angular
branches echo routed wires, while square and diamond pads suggest placed parts and connection
points. The roots continue the routing motif below the trunk.

Forest green carries the woodland theme; gold highlights selected terminals. The lowercase wordmark
has restrained, leaf-like curves that lend it an elvish character while keeping the name readable.

| Colour       | Hex       | Use                                           |
| ------------ | --------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Forest green | `#024823` | Mark and wordmark on light backgrounds; links |
| Antique gold | `#ba9015` | Terminals in the mark; accents                |
| Light sage   | `#ddeee0` | Replaces the green on dark backgrounds        |

The assets and their uses are listed in
[branding/README.md](https://github.com/Studio-Fug/yapnr/blob/main/branding/README.md). The logo
was created with an image generation tool on 2026-09-29; the prompt is kept as
`branding/yapnr-logo-prompt.txt` for provenance, and the story above is adapted from
`branding/BACKSTORY.txt`. The gardener-of-silicon framing is original to this project.
