yapnr — a circuit tree for a placement and routing engine

THE NAME

yapnr stands for “yet another place and route.” Its slightly fantastical sound
suggested a second, playful expansion: “Yavanna-Assisted Placement ’N Routing.”
That backronym was coined for this project.

The namesake is Yavanna, the figure in Tolkien’s legendarium associated with
growing things and the protection of plants. Her connection to trees inspired
the logo’s central idea: a tree whose branches are electronic routes.

THE STORY

Imagine a gardener of silicon. First, each cell finds its place; then paths
grow between them. A floorplan becomes a small, carefully tended forest of
connections. The engine’s practical work—placing cells and routing signals—
becomes the quiet craft of giving a circuit room to grow.

“Yet another place and route” remains the everyday expansion. The Yavanna
backronym gives the project a little woodland mythology to carry alongside it.

THE MARK

The emblem is a compact circuit-tree sigil. Its branching trunk suggests a Y
for yapnr. Angular branches echo routed wires, while square and diamond pads
suggest placed cells and connection points. The roots continue the routing
motif below the trunk.

Forest green carries the woodland theme; gold highlights selected terminals.
The lowercase wordmark has restrained, leaf-like curves that lend it an
elvish character while keeping the name readable.
